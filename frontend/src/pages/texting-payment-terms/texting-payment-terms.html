A separate 5% Polis service fee includes payment processing. The service fee and applicable taxes do not become texting funds.

Messaging charges

Polis passes through verified messaging costs without an additional per-message markup. Your organization's verified rates are shown in its texting workspace. The initial rates are:

  • Outgoing SMS, including messages sent as replies: $0.035 for up to two segments, plus $0.015 for each additional segment.
  • Outgoing MMS: $0.045 per complete message.
  • Received MMS: $0.05 per message.
  • Received SMS: actual carrier charges, only when their billing basis is disclosed and their cost is verified. There is no assumed fixed incoming SMS rate.

Text and encoding affect the number of SMS segments. Capacity estimates are estimates, not a fixed number of credits or a guarantee of delivery. Registration and number-rental fees are billed separately and are not deducted from these texting funds.

Controlled pilot

For organizations participating in the controlled pilot, Polis pays all automatic-response charges, including STOP and HELP responses, and other charges whose billing basis or amount remains unresolved. Those costs are not deducted from your organization's balance. They will not be charged retroactively. Any change to this pilot policy will be disclosed before it applies to future messaging.

Available funds and pending charges

Funds are reserved before an outgoing message is sent. Your available balance must cover that message's cost. Its reservation remains pending until the charge can be verified, including when the send outcome is uncertain. Final settlement accounts for the reservation without charging the same message twice.

Adding funds does not grant registration approval, remove a billing or provider hold, override an opt-out, confirm a queued message, or restart a campaign. New outgoing messages pause when funds are insufficient. Existing conversations, incoming messages and opt-out processing remain available as permitted by your organization's access and provider service. Disclosed incoming-message charges can continue while outgoing messages are paused.

Balance rules, refunds and support

Purchases are one-time payments, with no subscription or automatic refill. Balances belong to the purchasing organization, do not expire, and cannot be transferred, cashed out or used by another organization.

There are no routine refunds or customer self-service refunds. Contact support@polisapp.io for payment errors, disputes, or refunds required by law. Staff will review the request and record any authorized adjustment against the original payment. These terms do not limit rights required by law.

A reversal or dispute can reduce the balance or place outgoing messaging on a billing hold. Additional purchases do not automatically remove that hold. Contact support to resolve it.
