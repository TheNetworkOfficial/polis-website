Information sources

Government and election information sources

Polis is an independent civic-engagement platform operated by Lux Corp. Polis does not represent any government entity and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by any government.

Federal government information

Congressional members, federal bills, public laws, and House roll-call votes come from the U.S. Congress through the Congress.gov API: https://api.congress.gov/

Senate roll-call votes come from the United States Senate: https://www.senate.gov/legislative/votes_new.htm

Federal candidate registrations and campaign-finance records come from the Federal Election Commission: https://www.fec.gov/data/ and https://api.open.fec.gov/

Government geographic information

Address-to-district matching uses the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder: https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

Congressional and state legislative district boundaries come from the Census Bureau TIGERweb Legislative service: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Legislative/MapServer

State and county boundaries come from the Census Bureau TIGERweb State/County service: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/State_County/MapServer

County identifiers and names use the Census Bureau county reference data: https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes/files/national_county.txt

Candidate and election information by state

Montana candidate filings and rosters come from the Montana Secretary of State Candidate Filing service: https://candidatefiling.mt.gov/candidatefiling/CandidateHomepage.aspx

Montana election results come from the Secretary of State election-results portal and results archive: https://electionresults.mt.gov/ResultsList.aspx https://sosmt.gov/elections/results/

California election results come from the Secretary of State results feeds and certified-results archive: https://media.sos.ca.gov/media/json-endpoints.csv https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/prior-elections/statewide-election-results

Iowa election results come from the official statewide results portal: https://electionresults.iowa.gov/IA/

Coverage and limitations

Election-result coverage is limited to supported contests in Montana, California, and Iowa; it is not nationwide coverage. Data may be delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. Live election-night totals are preliminary, not certified final results. Consult the responsible election authority for official information.

The New Jersey, New Mexico, and South Dakota results integrations are not currently providing contest totals in the reviewed production configuration. They are not represented here as active result sources. Google Civic Information and CivicEngine are not the selected production district-election information provider; the selected provider is Congress.gov.

User and organization content

Community posts, candidate or organization statements, ratings, polls, and internal organization votes are supplied by users or organizations. They are not government statements or public-election ballots. General map imagery, address suggestions, and street imagery are navigation aids, distinct from the government district and election data identified above.
