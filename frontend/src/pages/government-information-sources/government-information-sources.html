Information sources

Government and election information sources

Polis is an independent civic-engagement platform operated by Lux Corp. This page identifies sources for government or election-related information that may appear in Polis.

Polis is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by any listed government, election authority, or third-party provider.

Federal and geographic sources

  • Congress: Congress API and Congress.gov
  • U.S. Senate legislative and roll-call information
  • Federal candidate and campaign finance: FEC API and FEC data
  • Congressional district and geographic information: Census TIGERweb and Census.gov
  • Election and polling-place information returned through Google Civic Information and Google Civic

Third-party civic data

CivicEngine may provide civic data enrichment through its API and website. CivicEngine is a third-party service, not a government agency or official election authority.

State and local election sources

Polis uses the applicable official source for the jurisdiction and election. Reviewed source pages include:

  • California Secretary of State
  • Iowa election results
  • Montana Secretary of State
  • New Jersey Division of Elections
  • New Mexico Secretary of State
  • South Dakota Secretary of State
  • Official state election-office directory

State and county feeds may be unavailable, delayed, incomplete, unofficial for live display, or limited to particular contests. Polis does not certify election results or declare winners.

User-generated material

Polis community polls, ratings, discussions, and “How would you vote?” responses are user-generated opinions. They are not government information, official votes, election results, or government statements.
