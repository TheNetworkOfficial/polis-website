Information sources

Government and election information sources

Polis is an independent civic-engagement platform operated by Lux Corp. Polis does not represent any government entity and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by any government.

Federal government information

Congressional members, federal bills, public laws, and House roll-call votes come from the U.S. Congress through the Congress.gov API: https://api.congress.gov/

Senate roll-call votes come from the United States Senate: https://www.senate.gov/legislative/votes_new.htm

Federal candidate registrations and campaign-finance records come from the Federal Election Commission: https://www.fec.gov/data/ and https://api.open.fec.gov/

Government geographic information

Address-to-district matching uses the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder: https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/

Congressional and state legislative district boundaries come from the Census Bureau TIGERweb Legislative service: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Legislative/MapServer

State and county boundaries come from the Census Bureau TIGERweb State/County service: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/State_County/MapServer

County identifiers and names use the Census Bureau county reference data: https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes/files/national_county.txt

Candidate and election information by state

Montana candidate filings and rosters come from the Montana Secretary of State Candidate Filing service: https://candidatefiling.mt.gov/candidatefiling/CandidateHomepage.aspx

Montana election results come from the Secretary of State election-results portal and results archive: https://electionresults.mt.gov/ResultsList.aspx https://sosmt.gov/elections/results/

California election results come from the Secretary of State results feeds and certified-results archive: https://media.sos.ca.gov/media/json-endpoints.csv https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/prior-elections/statewide-election-results

Iowa election results come from the official statewide results portal: https://electionresults.iowa.gov/IA/

Policy Questions: educational background and charts

Each published educational question links its background and chart to the specific sources below. Dates and units describe the underlying evidence. These explanatory charts are separate from answers submitted by Polis users.

Public-land sale rules and land changes

The U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel publishes 43 U.S.C. 1713. BLM explains sale procedures. The chart uses the FY2024 total row of BLM Public Land Statistics, Table 1-4: disposal/sales, acquisitions/exchanges/donations, restorations, and withdrawals/reservations. These are acreage activity categories, not opinion-poll results or land currently offered for sale.

  • U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel: 43 U.S.C. 1713 (public-land sales)
  • Bureau of Land Management: Sales and Exchanges
  • Bureau of Land Management: Public Land Statistics 2024, Table 1-4 (printed pages 11-13)

Pell Grant awards

The historical maximum scheduled awards for 2018-2019 through 2024-2025 come from Congressional Research Service Table B-1. The 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 amounts come from the Department of Education, Federal Student Aid letters below. Actual student awards depend on eligibility and enrollment; the chart is not an individual award estimate.

  • Congressional Research Service: Pell Grant historical maximum awards, Table B-1
  • Federal Student Aid: 2025-2026 Pell Grant award amounts
  • Federal Student Aid: 2026-2027 Pell Grant award amounts

Mental-health facility counts

SAMHSA's 2020 National Mental Health Services Survey, Table 5.1a (printed page 252), supplies inpatient counts with involuntary, non-forensic legal status on April 30, 2020. These are a one-day snapshot, not annual admissions. The chart shows selected facility types in the U.S. and its jurisdictions; the residential-treatment category combines children and adults.

  • SAMHSA: 2020 National Mental Health Services Survey, Table 5.1a (printed page 252)

State approaches to election deepfakes

Utah's Office of Legislative Research and General Counsel briefing of August 19, 2026, pages 3-4, reproduces NCSL's June 2026 tally of 28 disclosure states and three prohibition states. These are historical enacted-law classifications; some provisions were blocked by courts. NCSL is a nongovernment research organization, separately identified below.

  • Utah Office of Legislative Research and General Counsel: Artificial Intelligence in Elections (August 19, 2026), pages 3-4
  • National Conference of State Legislatures: Deepfakes in Elections and Campaigns (June 23, 2026; non-government research)

Stablecoins: statutory framework and reserve composition

The U.S. Government Publishing Office publishes the GENIUS Act, Public Law 119-27. Federal Reserve FEDS Notes, Figure 1, supplies the Tether reserve shares for June 30, 2025, calculated by its authors from issuer transparency reports. The chart is historical research, not a government endorsement or a statement of current issuer compliance.

  • U.S. Government Publishing Office: GENIUS Act, Public Law 119-27
  • Federal Reserve FEDS Notes: reserve composition, Figure 1

Medicare-for-All public opinion: independent research

KFF is an independent, nongovernment health-policy research organization. Its April 11-16, 2019 Health Tracking Poll provides the chart: 56% favor, 38% oppose, 6% do not know/refused. The telephone survey included 1,203 U.S. adults; its overall sampling margin of error was plus or minus three percentage points. The results concern the poll's stated national health-plan proposal, not current opinion or the answer choices offered to Polis users.

  • KFF Health Tracking Poll, April 11-16, 2019: methodology and M4ALL topline (independent research)

Coverage and limitations

Election-result coverage is limited to supported contests in Montana, California, and Iowa; it is not nationwide coverage. Data may be delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. Live election-night totals are preliminary, not certified final results. Consult the responsible election authority for official information.

The New Jersey, New Mexico, and South Dakota results integrations are not currently providing contest totals in the reviewed production configuration. They are not represented here as active result sources. Google Civic Information and CivicEngine are not the selected production district-election information provider; the selected provider is Congress.gov.

User and organization content

Community posts, candidate or organization statements, ratings, polls, and internal organization votes are supplied by users or organizations. They are not government statements or public-election ballots. General map imagery, address suggestions, and street imagery are navigation aids, distinct from the government district and election data identified above.
