Organization Geocoding Retention and Closure Policy

Version: geocoding-retention-2026-10-27-beta-1. Document date: October 27, 2026. Effective upon publication. Operator: Lux Corp, 4700 3rd Ave S, Great Falls, MT 59405. Privacy requests: lux.corp.app@gmail.com. Payment support: lux@polisapp.io.

1. Principles and scope

Original uploads and the organization's working contact book remain separate from Polis's licensed address/geocode copies. Only a qualifying successful save after genuine acceptance grants the limited geocoding license. A continuing license permits continuing eligible reuse; it does not require retaining every copy forever. No scheduled cache expiry requires repurchasing a geocode merely because time passed. Accuracy, source restrictions, valid privacy requests and current necessity can require a correction, restricted use or removal.

The periods below are the approved category-specific retention rules. They do not state universal legal deadlines or replace a provider's own retention. Records selected for service use remain subject to the applicable operational controls and documented exceptions. A shorter applicable legal requirement controls. A longer legal hold or mandatory record requirement must identify the affected records, reason, owner and review date; it does not justify retaining unrelated contact information.

3. Deletion, correction and restrictions

Polis verifies the requester, relevant organization authority and scope. An organization's deletion of its working contact is different from an individual request to remove or restrict retained licensed copies. A narrow request does not authorize deleting another person's information, another organization's original file, or unrelated financial/acceptance records.

The service targets are acknowledgement within 5 business days and completion or a documented status/extension within 30 calendar days after verification, unless an applicable requirement is sooner. Provider or backup work still outstanding must be identified honestly. Do not label a request complete merely because a working contact was removed.

Review the matching licensed grants, contribution records, working geocode copies, provider evidence, exports and backups. Remove/restrict affected copies and preserve independent valid contributions where appropriate. An independent contribution is not a reason to ignore a restriction applying to the address itself. Keep the organization or affected operation paused until future and queued work cannot recreate a prohibited copy.

4. Restores and delayed work

Suppression and contributor-revocation evidence must be retained separately from the backup being restored. Before an isolated restoration can serve requests, replay current restrictions, remove the selected erased field values/cache copies and verify that delayed outboxes and stored provider results cannot recreate them. Preserve valid independent contributions without clearing an address-level privacy restriction.

Restoring a snapshot does not restore permission to spend, renew subscriptions, license data or run organization automation. Reconcile authoritative payment, acceptance, policy and suppression state before enabling the restored service.

5. Closure, reopening and permanent discontinuation

Closure pauses service access and new processing, disables automation, and must stop future subscription renewal through the supported cancellation process. Already issued work and payment events remain subject to reconciliation. Closure does not create an elective cash refund, cash-out right, scheduled credit expiry or forfeiture of purchased credits.

The 90-day recovery period concerns the organization's original/working content; it does not limit the life of its purchased-credit balance. Reopening requires fresh verification of the same organization and its current owner/administrator, current required acceptance and available service. Restore an eligible retained balance after reconciliation. Do not restart a cancelled paid subscription or automation without a new express instruction. Content already lawfully purged after the recovery period is not promised to be recoverable.

If the geocoding service permanently discontinues, stop new sales, preserve balance/transaction evidence, provide at least 60 days' notice where practicable or any greater required notice, and provide a reasonable opportunity to use credits or export eligible organization content. Do not promise use where the service cannot safely continue. Resolve remaining obligations under applicable mandatory refund, payment and unclaimed-property rules; do not automatically write off balances. A specific discontinuation notice and handling plan must be reviewed against the circumstances before it is issued.

6. Geocodio and Stripe

Provider retention is governed by the actual account and contract. The Privacy Supplement describes this release's Geocodio Self-Serve request/log handling and the manual provider request process. Polis's right to retain outputs does not mean Geocodio keeps no request data. No provider deletion endpoint or immediate completion guarantee is promised.

Stripe keeps its own records under its applicable terms, privacy notices and obligations. Polis stores the limited payment evidence it needs; this policy does not direct Stripe to erase a record or promise erasure from its systems.

7. Scope and updates

This schedule applies to the initial website geocoding release for Courage for Democracy (C4D) and Cascade County Democratic Central Committee (Cascade County Democrats). It does not expand the licensed fields or authorize historical migration. Existing protected rollback evidence and unrelated Governance, messaging and Text Banking retention remain outside this schedule.

A material change to this policy is versioned and subject to applicable notice and acceptance requirements. The Geocoding Addendum and Subscription and Credit Terms continue to govern the limited license, spending authority and purchased-credit rules. Adopting this schedule does not itself perform a deletion, start a pilot, purchase a service or enable organization automation.
