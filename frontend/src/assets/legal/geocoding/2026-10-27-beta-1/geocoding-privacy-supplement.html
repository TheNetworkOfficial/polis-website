Privacy Supplement for Organization Geocoding

Version: geocoding-privacy-2026-10-27-beta-1. Document date: October 27, 2026. Effective upon publication and applicable organization acceptance.

Operator: Lux Corp. Legal-notice address: 4700 3rd Ave S, Great Falls, MT 59405. Privacy contact: lux.corp.app@gmail.com.

This supplement describes the initial organization geocoding release and supplements the Polis Privacy Policy. The initial participating workspaces are Courage for Democracy (C4D) and Cascade County Democratic Central Committee (Cascade County Democrats). It does not expand licensing to texting, contact editing, field observations, or historical processing.

Addresses, geocodes, and organization separation

When a participating organization requests geocoding, Polis processes the address components needed to find a location and stores the result and supporting resolution information. Where a suitable licensed result is available, Polis may reuse it. Otherwise, Polis sends the required address components to Geocodio for processing. The request excludes contact names, phone numbers, political affiliations, notes, and the original contact file.

Under the organization's accepted geocoding addendum, Polis keeps a separate licensed address/geocode copy for the stated geocoding purposes. Another organization can receive a matching geocode only after independently supplying the address. That reuse does not disclose the original organization, its contact book, or other contact fields. Internal provenance records document the source, processing action, acceptance, and any restrictions; they are not available as a cross-organization contact directory.

Purchases and subscriptions

Stripe processes website credit and subscription payments. Payment records include the organization's purchase, plan, and transaction information. Purchasing geocode credits or a plan does not send the organization's contact book to Stripe. Polis retains appropriate transaction and acceptance records for accounting, security, dispute handling, and legal requirements. No card is required for the initial complimentary Campaign offer, and it does not automatically convert to a paid plan.

Retention and providers

Polis and its providers retain information according to applicable retention schedules, contracts, and legal requirements. An organization's deletion of a working contact or account closure is distinct from a verified privacy erasure across retained copies. A continuing address/geocode license remains subject to applicable rights and retention limits.

The Organization Geocoding Retention and Closure Policy lists separate periods and review rules for originals where retained, licensed address/geocode copies, provenance, acceptance/payment records, temporary exports, backups and suppression. The Geocoding Addendum sets out the limited license, and Subscription and Credit Terms describe billing and purchased balances.

The provider account for this release is Geocodio Self-Serve pay-as-you-go. Geocodio's published retention policy keeps detailed request logs for 45 days and distinguishes query retention by endpoint. Polis currently sends POST v2 requests as a single-element batch. Polis does not promise that these address inputs are never retained. Provider privacy/deletion requests follow the provider's available manual support process; any remaining handling or retention is tracked until evidenced. Immediate erasure from provider systems is not guaranteed. See Geocodio's retention policy and provider terms.

Provider retention is distinct from Polis's permission to retain a geocode. Polis does not promise that a provider keeps no request data or that an external record is erased immediately without verified evidence.

Requests and safeguards

Send privacy, correction, or deletion requests to lux.corp.app@gmail.com. Polis verifies requests and assesses applicable retained copies, aliases, providers, exports, and backups. Responses protect other individuals' information; an individual request does not entitle the requester to another organization's entire original voter file or internal provenance.

Where required, Polis removes affected contributions and prevents erased data from returning through delayed processing or restored backups. Independently authorized contributions may support continued retention of an address-only result. Minimal suppression, transaction, or legal-hold records may remain where necessary and permitted. Privacy cleanup does not remove an outreach refusal or permit previously prohibited contact.
