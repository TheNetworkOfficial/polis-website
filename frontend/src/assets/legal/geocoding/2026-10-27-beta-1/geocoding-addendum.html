Organization Geocoding Addendum

Version: geocoding-addendum-2026-10-27-beta-1. Document date: October 27, 2026. Effective upon publication and applicable organization acceptance.

Legal-notice address: 4700 3rd Ave S, Great Falls, MT 59405.

This addendum is between Lux Corp, operator of Polis, and the organization identified in the acceptance record. It supplements the Polis Terms of Service and governs the geocoding and data-use matters described here if those documents conflict.

1. Initial service

The initial release provides manual address geocoding through the Polis website, optional automatic geocoding, monthly geocoding subscriptions, and website purchases of geocode credits. Availability is limited to the approved Courage for Democracy (C4D) and Cascade County Democratic Central Committee (Cascade County Democrats) workspaces. The registered organization names are those verified in their acceptance records. Access remains subject to organization permissions, available credits, supported addresses, and service availability. Native-app spending, field preauthorization, and historical licensing are not part of this initial release.

2. Original materials

The organization retains its rights in uploaded files and information. Polis may store and process those materials to provide the organization's services, secure the platform, and comply with law. Uploading, parsing, indexing, viewing, or navigating a contact book does not grant Polis independent reuse rights over the entire file. Original materials remain organization-exclusive for this purpose. Where an exact original is archived, it is kept separately from the licensed address/geocode copy. Polis does not represent parsed legacy rows as an original file where the original bytes were not retained.

3. Limited address-and-geocode license

When an authorized geocoding operation successfully saves a result after this addendum is accepted, the organization grants Polis a nonexclusive, royalty-free, continuing license to retain and use a separate copy of the address components used, the resulting coordinates, and associated resolution-quality and provenance information. Polis may use that copy to provide, maintain, validate, and improve its address-geocoding service and supply matching geocodes to other authorized organizations.

Cross-organization reuse requires the requesting organization independently to provide the matching address. It does not disclose the contributing organization, its contact book, source file, contact identities, or internal contribution history. The organization retains its own rights; this license does not transfer ownership of its original file or purport to create ownership of facts.

4. Excluded information and activities

This initial addendum does not grant independent reuse rights over names, telephone numbers, email addresses, political affiliations, messages, notes, or field observations merely because they appear alongside an address. Texting, contact editing, DoorKnocker activity, and operational field handoffs do not create additional licenses under this initial release. Expansion to those activities requires separately approved terms and acceptance.

5. Prospective operation

This license applies only to qualifying geocoding saves after acceptance. Previously uploaded addresses may be processed afterward with authorization, but acceptance does not retrospectively license previous processing or the remainder of an uploaded file. No historical migration is authorized by this addendum. Signing this addendum does not itself start geocoding; an authorized manual instruction or separate automatic-geocoding opt-in is required.

6. Authority and source restrictions

The organization represents that it has the rights and authority necessary to provide the addresses, authorize geocoding, and grant the limited license above. It must disclose applicable restrictions and must not submit data whose restrictions prohibit the requested use. This addendum does not waive individual rights or override applicable source restrictions or law.

7. Providers and retention

Polis may send the address components needed for an external lookup to Geocodio. The geocoding request does not include the contact's name, phone number, political affiliation, or original contact file. Provider processing and retention are described in the accompanying privacy supplement.

Polis's licensed address/geocode copy may remain after an organization deletes its working contact or closes its account, subject to applicable privacy rights, approved retention rules, and legal holds. A continuing license does not require indefinite retention of every copy. The Organization Geocoding Retention and Closure Policy sets the category-specific schedule, review procedures and treatment of closure, privacy requests and backups. The Privacy Supplement describes related processing and provider handling.

8. Requests and acceptance

Privacy, correction, and deletion requests may be sent to lux.corp.app@gmail.com. Polis verifies requests and assesses retained copies, independent contributions, provider records, and backup suppression as applicable. Minimal records may remain where necessary to honor restrictions, document transactions, or meet legal requirements.

The signer confirms authority to bind the organization. Polis records the signer's name and title, authority attestation, document version and hash, organization legal-profile revision, platform, and acceptance time. A changed legal profile requires renewed acceptance. A document update expanding these licensed purposes requires a new version and acceptance.
