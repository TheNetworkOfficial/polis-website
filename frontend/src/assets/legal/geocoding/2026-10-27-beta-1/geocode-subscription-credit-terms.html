3. Plan changes, cancellation, and failed renewal

An upgrade takes effect immediately only after the applicable prorated payment is verified. Its daily allowance becomes the new plan's target allowance; the old and new allowances are not added together. Credits already used or reserved for that day count toward the target. The amount payable is shown before the upgrade is confirmed.

Downgrades and cancellation take effect at the end of the current paid billing period. Cancelling prevents the next renewal; it does not create a discretionary refund for the current period. New included-credit grants remain governed by the current paid plan until its paid-through time. Daily credits already issued retain their disclosed 00:00 UTC expiry; a midday paid-period end does not confiscate that bucket.

There is no renewal grace period. If renewal payment has not been verified at the end of the paid-through period, new included-credit grants stop then. A pending, failed, or disputed payment does not extend the paid-through period. Purchased credits remain on the organization's balance and may be used where the account and service remain eligible and are not subject to a billing or other applicable hold.

5. Credit charges and reservations

One credit acquires an eligible reusable geocode; two credits authorize an external lookup. Multiple contacts sharing the same exact address share one acquisition. Unit identifiers form part of address identity. Already located contacts and addresses already acquired by the organization do not incur another acquisition charge.

A manual confirmation states the maximum authorized credit amount. Polis reserves that amount before processing and settles credits when an authorized usable result is saved. Unsaved unsuccessful work releases its unspent reservation. Expired included credits do not become available again merely because a reservation is released. The final charge may be lower than the approved maximum. Cancelling work stops subsequent processing; completed saves remain charged and already issued work may need settlement or review.

6. Automatic geocoding

An authorized administrator may enable automatic geocoding of eligible unlocated contacts. In this initial release, automation uses included credits only; it cannot spend purchased credits. An administrator may pause automation. Manual operations can use purchased credits after available included credits. Any later automatic use of purchased credits requires a separately disclosed opt-in and daily purchased-credit cap; this release grants no such authority. Ordinary map views and navigation do not spend credits.

7. Errors, refunds, and disputes

Purchases are final. Polis does not offer discretionary refunds for a change of mind, unused credits, partial use, plan cancellation or downgrade, cancellation of automation, or voluntary account closure. Refunds are provided only where required by applicable law.

This policy does not permit Polis to retain money it was not entitled to collect. Contact lux@polisapp.io about duplicate or erroneous charges, payments collected without delivery of the purchased entitlement or credits, unauthorized transactions, or refunds required by law. Verified errors will be corrected through the appropriate payment or credit adjustment, including returning improperly collected money where required. Releasing a failed geocoding reservation is a credit-accounting correction, not a discretionary cash refund.

Nothing in these terms waives mandatory consumer, payment-card, dispute, or other legal rights or requires contacting Polis before exercising them. A reversal or dispute may reduce purchased credits or place new spending on hold while the affected payment is reconciled. Polis records adjustments against the original transaction and does not recover the same credits twice.

8. Closure and suspension

Closing or suspending an organization stops access and further processing. Closure does not create a discretionary refund or cash-out entitlement and does not itself expire or automatically forfeit purchased credits. Polis retains the balance and transaction records for reconciliation and treatment required by law. Credits cannot be spent while the account is closed or suspended. Any later reopening and use remains subject to authorization and service availability.

The Organization Geocoding Retention and Closure Policy describes the 90-day content-recovery period, reopening and permanent discontinuation. That recovery period does not expire purchased credits. The Geocoding Addendum and Privacy Supplement describe the separate address/geocode license and processing.

9. Service limits and notices

A geocode is an estimated location. Polis does not guarantee that every address can be located or that every result is accurate. Purchasing credits does not override source restrictions, privacy requests, permissions, billing holds, or the requirement for valid organization acceptance. Changes to the price, allowance, or renewal terms require the applicable notice and purchase/renewal authorization.
